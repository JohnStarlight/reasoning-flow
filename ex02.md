# Step-by-Step Reasoning Prompt

## Prompt:
Calculate the factorial of 5 but let's think step by step before answering.

## Answer:
```python?code_reference&code_event_index=2
import math
print(math.factorial(5))

```
```text?code_stdout&code_event_index=2

```
To calculate the factorial of 5 (denoted as $5!$), we follow the mathematical definition: the product of all positive integers from 1 up to the number itself.

Here is the step-by-step breakdown of the calculation:

1.  **Identify the sequence**: For $5!$, we need to multiply the integers 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5.
    $$5! = 5 \times 4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1$$

2.  **Multiply the first two numbers**:
    $$5 \times 4 = 20$$

3.  **Multiply the result by the next number (3)**:
    $$20 \times 3 = 60$$

4.  **Multiply that result by the next number (2)**:
    $$60 \times 2 = 120$$

5.  **Multiply by the final number (1)**:
    $$120 \times 1 = 120$$



**Final Answer:**
The factorial of 5 is **120**.
