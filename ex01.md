# Logic Puzzle with Explanation

## Prompt:
Alice is older than Bob, Bob is older than Carol. Who is the youngest?
Explain your reasoning before giving the final answer.

## Answer:
To figure out who is the youngest, we can look at the relationships as a simple chain of age.

### The Reasoning
1.  **First Comparison:** We are told **Alice is older than Bob**. This means Bob is younger than Alice.
2.  **Second Comparison:** We are told **Bob is older than Carol**. This means Carol is younger than Bob.
3.  **The Chain:** If Alice is older than Bob, and Bob is older than Carol, we can arrange them in order from oldest to youngest like this:
    * **Alice > Bob > Carol**

Since Carol is the person at the very end of that "older than" chain, she must be the one with the lowest age.

---

### The Final Answer
**Carol** is the youngest.
